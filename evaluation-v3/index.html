    Evaluation v3 (plan §60)¶

    The plan's v3 evaluation table, filled in as each area is built, and including what does not flatter the project. A row says what was measured, on what, and what it does not show; a row with nothing measured says "not measured" and why. Phase 14 (Weeks 57-59) is the first to contribute.

    What the Phase 14 numbers do not show¶

    • Agreement with DefectDojo is agreement with DefectDojo's tests, not with ground truth. Where Bulwark differs on purpose (a finding per place, licence findings not imported, an empty Nuclei file closing nothing) the difference is in the test file with its reason.
    • The ingest numbers are throughput on a laptop for files built by repeating real records; the whole import peaks at 1.5 GB for 117,000 SARIF findings, and nothing was run at the plan's other scale (one million Occurrences) here.
    • Fuzzing found one real bug (memory, not a crash) in minutes of running; that is evidence the harness works, not that the parsers are clean.
