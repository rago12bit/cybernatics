    Compliance mappings, the ASVS checklist and evidence packs¶

    Bulwark shows where the evidence it holds supports a framework's controls. It is not a certification, an audit opinion or a statement of compliance, and no screen or file says it is. The design is ADR-0053.

    Mappings¶

    One versioned file per framework in core/compliance/frameworks/, each citing its source, version and terms, and mapping each control to the kinds of evidence Bulwark holds:

    Framework Controls Source and terms
    OWASP Top 10:2021 10 categories, each with OWASP's own CWE list OWASP, CC BY-SA 4.0
    OWASP ASVS 4.0.3 278 requirements with level and CWE OWASP, CC BY-SA 4.0, not reworded
    PCI DSS v4.0, Requirement 6 6.1.1 to 6.5.1 PCI SSC; numbers and Bulwark's paraphrases only
    ISO/IEC 27001:2022, Annex A 8.8 "Management of technical vulnerabilities" ISO/IEC; the control's name only
    SOC 2 TSC (2017, rev. 2022), CC7.1 Detection and monitoring of vulnerabilities AICPA; Bulwark's paraphrase only

    The kinds of evidence: scan coverage, web (DAST) scans, SLA adherence, risk acceptances, open Issues, open Issues of a control's CWEs, the audit-log extract, the component inventory, advisory monitoring, VEX statements, assessments, and the policies in force. Some controls have none (developer training, a web application firewall, payment-page scripts) and say so.

    GET /v2/compliance/frameworks
GET /v2/compliance/frameworks/{id}

    The ASVS checklist of a project¶

    Project → ASVS: every ASVS 4.0.3 requirement up to level 1, 2 or 3, with a status:

    Status Set by Means
    Met / Not met / Not applicable A person, with a note (required for met and not applicable) and links What the person decided
    Not met An assessment that failed the requirement A tester's result
    Issues open Open Issues of the requirement's CWE Evidence against it
    Passed a test An assessment that passed it Evidence for it, not "met"
    Not assessed Nothing No evidence either way

    Bulwark never marks a requirement met: no findings is not evidence that a control works. A person's "met" that open Issues or a failed test contradict is flagged.

    GET /v2/projects/{id}/asvs?level=2
PUT /v2/projects/{id}/asvs/{requirement}   {"status": "met", "note": "...", "links": [...]}

    Evidence packs¶

    Evidence in the top bar: pick a period (up to 400 days) and frameworks, see what will be included, generate. The pack is a zip:

    File What
    evidence.pdf The pack to read
    evidence.json The same, machine-readable
    audit-extract.json The audit log for the period: your entries in full, others' as hashes
    checkpoints/ Each Rekor checkpoint's Sigstore bundle
    verify.py Checks it all without trusting Bulwark (Python 3, standard library only)
    manifest.json, manifest.json.sig Every file's SHA-256, and cosign's signature over it
    audit.pub, trusted-root.json When the deployment provides them: the checkpoint key and a private Rekor's trust root

    What it reports, and how:

    • SLA adherence: Issues whose deadline fell in the period, under the SLA policy in force at its end. Met: fixed by the deadline. Missed: fixed after. Open past deadline: not fixed by the period's end. Exempt: marked not a risk or accepted (as triaged now). Adherence = met / (met + missed + open past deadline).
    • Risk acceptances requested, decided or in force in the period, with requester, approver and expiry, and whether a second person approved.
    • Scan coverage per project: full and pull-request scans, web scans and imported reports in the period, the tools, the last scan.
    • Open Issues by priority, as of generation (not as of the period's end).
    • Components, advisories, VEX, assessments and SLA/priority policies.
    • For each framework, each control with the evidence in the pack for it.

    Checking a pack¶

    unzip evidence-pack.zip -d pack && cd pack && python3 verify.py

    verify.py checks every file against manifest.json, recomputes the audit chain (every entry's hash, every link), and checks that each checkpoint names a hash that is in the extract at that position. It then prints the two cosign commands it cannot run itself: the signature on the manifest (with the organization's public key, obtained from the organization, not only from the pack) and each checkpoint's signature and Rekor entry.

    Signing and Rekor¶

    • BULWARK_EVIDENCE_SIGNING_KEY (a cosign private key; its password in COSIGN_PASSWORD) signs packs. Without it, packs are generated and marked unsigned; with it, a signing failure produces no pack.
    • bulwark audit checkpoint --key cosign.key [--rekor-url URL] publishes the chain's head to Rekor: Sigstore's public instance (permanent and public) by default, or a private one.
    • bulwark audit verify --pub cosign.pub [--trusted-root trusted-root.json] checks the whole chain and every checkpoint's signature and Rekor entry; with a trusted root (cosign trusted-root create for a private Rekor) it needs no network.
    • BULWARK_AUDIT_CHECKPOINT_PUB and BULWARK_REKOR_TRUSTED_ROOT add the checkpoint key and trust root to packs.

    Why another organization's entries are hashes¶

    The audit log is one chain for the whole deployment. Since migration 0041 each entry's organization is inside its hash, through a digest of the entry's body, so a pack can show its organization's entries in full and every other entry as (seq, prev_hash, body_digest, entry_hash): every hash still recomputes, and nothing about the other entry is revealed except that it exists. Entries from before 0041 have no organization; they are shown as hashes and checked by link only.
